Notion can be anything, which is exactly why most student setups collapse by
week three — they try to be everything. A setup that lasts a whole semester is
built from a small number of databases that each do one job well. Here's the
blueprint.

## The four databases you actually need

1. **Assignments** — every task with a course, due date, and status. This is
   the engine; see our
   [assignment tracker guide](/articles/notion-assignment-tracker/) for the
   exact properties.
2. **Courses** — one row per class: professor, meeting times, room, and a link
   to that course's notes. Everything else can *relate* back to this.
3. **Notes** — one page per lecture or topic, tagged by course. Nothing fancy;
   the goal is that you can find them later.
4. **Schedule** — your weekly classes and commitments as a calendar.

That's it. Habit trackers, reading lists, and budgets are nice, but add them
*after* the core four are a daily habit — not before.

## How they connect

The one relationship worth setting up: link **Assignments** and **Notes** to
**Courses** with a Notion *relation* property. Now opening a course page shows
everything for that class in one place — upcoming work, past notes, logistics.
This is the difference between a pile of pages and a system.

## The dashboard page

Create one page called **Home** and embed *filtered views* of your databases
as linked views:

- Assignments due this week
- Today's classes from Schedule
- Recently edited Notes

Pin **Home** to the top of your sidebar. This one page becomes the thing you
open every morning.

## What to skip (for now)

- **Elaborate templates with 15 databases** — you'll spend more time
  maintaining the system than studying.
- **Formulas and rollups** on day one — add them when you hit a real
  limitation, not preemptively.
- **Someone else's aesthetic** copied button-by-button — copy the *structure*,
  not the decoration.

## Getting it aesthetic without the work

The structure above is 90% of the value; the last 10% — cover images, icons,
consistent colors, a layout that's actually pleasant to open — is where hours
disappear. If you want the four-database structure *and* the polished aesthetic
layout already done, duplicate the template below and spend your time studying
instead of styling.
